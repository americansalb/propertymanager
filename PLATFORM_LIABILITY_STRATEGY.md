# PropertyMaster Platform Liability Strategy
## Marketplace Verification Without Assuming Employer Liability

### Legal Position: PropertyMaster is a MARKETPLACE, not an employer

**Key Principle**: We verify compliance with state law. We do NOT make independent determinations of who is "qualified" - the state licensing boards do that.

---

## VERIFICATION STRATEGY (Liability-Minimizing)

### ✅ WHAT WE DO VERIFY:

#### 1. **State-Issued License Verification**
**Why**: State licensing boards already conduct background checks, fingerprinting, and criminal history review as part of licensing.

**State Law Examples**:

**California** (Business & Professions Code § 7068.1):
- Contractors State License Board requires:
  - Fingerprinting through Live Scan
  - DOJ background check
  - FBI background check
  - Disqualifying offenses listed in B&P Code § 480

**Texas** (Occupations Code § 1702.113) - Locksmith:
- DPS background check required
- Criminal history evaluation
- Disqualifying offenses specified

**Florida** (Chapter 489) - Contractors:
- Level 2 background screening required
- FDLE and FBI fingerprinting
- Disqualifying offenses under § 435.04

**Our Action**: Verify license is ACTIVE and IN GOOD STANDING with state board
**Liability**: State board determines qualification, not us

---

#### 2. **Insurance Coverage Verification**
**Why**: Transfers liability to insurance carrier

**What We Verify**:
- Certificate of Insurance (COI) is current and valid
- Coverage meets minimum thresholds ($500K general liability)
- Policy covers the work they'll be doing
- Named insured matches business registration

**Our Action**: Call insurance agent to verify coverage if API unavailable
**Liability**: Insurance carrier assumes liability for covered incidents

---

#### 3. **Business Entity Verification**
**Why**: Confirms they're a legitimate registered business

**What We Verify**:
- EIN matches IRS records (TIN Match API)
- Business registered with Secretary of State
- Business entity is active
- Address is physical location (not PO box)

**Our Action**: Verify with government databases
**Liability**: We verified they're a real business, state verified they're legal

---

#### 4. **State Licensing Law Compliance**
**Why**: We verify they meet STATE requirements, not our own arbitrary standards

**What We Track**:
- Which states require licensing for each trade
- License types required by state
- Minimum insurance requirements by state
- Bonding requirements (if state-mandated)

**Our Action**: Maintain database of state requirements, verify vendor meets them
**Liability**: We're verifying compliance with state law

---

### ❌ WHAT WE DO NOT DO:

#### 1. **Independent Background Checks**
**Why NOT**:
- Creates liability if we miss something the state didn't
- Implies we're making "hiring" decisions (we're not - we're a marketplace)
- Duplicates state licensing board background checks
- Could expose us to discrimination claims

**Instead**: We verify the state license is active (state already did background check)

---

#### 2. **Make Independent Qualification Determinations**
**Why NOT**:
- We're not the licensing authority
- Creates liability for "negligent credentialing"
- Implies employer relationship

**Instead**: We verify state licensing board says they're qualified

---

#### 3. **Guarantee Work Quality or Safety**
**Why NOT**:
- We're a marketplace, not a contracting company
- Creates warranty liability
- Implies we're the employer

**Instead**: Our Terms of Service clearly state:
- Vendors are independent contractors
- Property managers select and supervise vendors
- We verify state licensing and insurance only

## VERIFICATION WORKFLOW (Liability-Minimizing)

### Step 1: Vendor Submits Application
**We collect**:
- State license number
- License state
- License expiration date
- Insurance carrier and policy number
- Insurance expiration date
- EIN
- Business registration details

### Step 2: Automated Verification
**We verify with state databases**:
- License is ACTIVE (not suspended, revoked, expired)
- No disciplinary actions
- Insurance is current (call agent if needed)
- Business is registered with state

### Step 3: Admin Review
**Admin checks**:
- All verifications passed
- No red flags (mismatched names, expired docs, etc.)
- State requirements met for that trade

### Step 4: Approval or Rejection
**If approved**: Vendor can receive job offers
**If rejected**: Vendor notified of specific deficiency (expired license, no insurance, etc.)

### Step 5: Ongoing Monitoring
**We monitor**:
- License expiration (30-day warning, auto-suspend at expiration)
- Insurance expiration (30-day warning, auto-suspend at expiration)
- Disciplinary actions (monthly check with state boards)

**We do NOT**:
- Re-run background checks
- Make independent fitness determinations
- Supervise their work

## WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

### If Vendor Causes Damage/Injury:

**Our Legal Position**:
1. Vendor is independent contractor (not our employee)
2. We verified state license was active (state did background check)
3. We verified insurance coverage (carrier assumes liability)
4. Property manager selected and supervised vendor
5. We are marketplace, not employer or general contractor

**Our Response**:
1. Immediately re-verify license status (was it active at time of incident?)
2. Verify insurance was active (refer to carrier)
3. Provide documentation of our verification
4. Refer property manager to vendor's insurance carrier
5. Suspend vendor if license/insurance lapsed

### If State Revokes Vendor License:

**Our Response**:
1. Immediately suspend vendor account
2. Notify property managers with active jobs
3. Remove from search results
4. Document timeline of our verification

**Our Legal Position**: We verified license was active when we listed them. State board is authority for license revocation.
