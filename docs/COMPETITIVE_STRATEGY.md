## 2. What We Are (and Are Not)

### We Are

- A **software platform** for small–mid landlords (1–50 units)
- Focused on:
  - **Automation** (collections, maintenance, renewals)
  - **Predictability** (cashflow, risk scores, forecasts)
  - **Affordability** ($10/unit/month, transparent pricing)

### We Are Not

- We are **not** a rent-guarantee fund or master-lease operator.
- We do **not** take balance-sheet risk on rent payments (at least in v1).
- We do **not** market blanket "guaranteed rent" without clear legal/financial backing.

---

## 3. Position vs "Guaranteed Rent" Competitors

### Their Promise

- "Guaranteed rent even if the tenant doesn't pay"
- 8–10% of monthly rent
- Many conditions in the fine print (pricing control, screening rules, property condition, caps on vacancy days, etc.)

### Our Counter-Position

> **Predictable income without giving away 8–10% of your rent.**

We focus on:

1. **Higher on-time collection**
   - Auto-pay (Phase 2)
   - Smart reminders (Phase 1)
   - Late fee automation (Phase 1)
   - Proactive workflows for at-risk tenants (Phase 4A)

2. **Lower vacancy and better pricing**
   - AI pricing & renewal recommendations (Phase 4A)
   - Vacancy and delinquency analytics (Phase 2)

3. **Radical transparency**
   - Real-time dashboard: on-time %, delinquency, vacancy days, cashflow
   - No fuzzy "guarantee" language; just numbers.

### The Math

On a $2,000/month property:
- **Belong's guarantee:** ~$160-200/month (8-10% of rent) = **$1,920-2,400/year**
- **PropertyMaster:** $10/month = **$120/year**
- **Savings:** **$1,800-2,280/year per property**

**Our message:** "Stop paying $2,000/year for 'guaranteed rent.' Start collecting rent faster, automatically, for $120/year."

## 4. Future: Partnered Rent Protection (Phase 6+)

We may support **optional** rent-protection via licensed partners (e.g., rent guarantee insurance) once:

- We have payment history at scale
- We can price risk accurately
- We have the legal/compliance pieces ready

Positioning:

- Clearly labeled as **"Rent Protection via Partner"**
- Optional per property / lease
- Transparent fee (e.g., 4–6% of rent), not hidden in marketing.

## 7. Handling "Do you offer guaranteed rent?" (Sales Script)

**Question:** "Do you offer guaranteed rent like Belong/Nomad?"

**Answer:**

> "We don't currently offer a blanket rent guarantee. Here's why:
>
> Most 'guaranteed rent' programs cost 8-10% of your monthly rent - that's $160-200/month on a $2,000 property, or about $2,000/year. They also often require you to accept their pricing, screening, and property standards.
>
> Instead, PropertyMaster focuses on making non-payment rare through automation:
> - Auto-pay gets 95-98% on-time collection
> - Smart screening reduces tenant risk upfront
> - Automated late fees and reminders handle the remaining 2-5%
>
> You keep 100% of your rent, pay $10/month, and maintain control of your property.
>
> For owners who truly need a 100% guarantee for financing or risk reasons, we're planning optional rent-protection partnerships in Phase 6+ - clearly labeled and transparently priced at 4-6% instead of 8-10%."

## 8. Target Customer Profile

### Who Chooses Us

- **Cost-conscious landlords** who value keeping their margins
- **Hands-on owners** who want control, not a middleman
- **Data-driven operators** who prefer metrics over marketing promises
- **Growth-focused investors** building 5-50 unit portfolios

### Who Chooses Guaranteed Rent

- **Risk-averse owners** who need guaranteed cashflow for debt service
- **Passive investors** who want zero management involvement
- **Owners with problem properties** needing quick guaranteed income
- **Institutional owners** with fiduciary requirements for guaranteed returns

### Our Strategy

We're **not trying to convert the guaranteed-rent customer**. We're targeting the 80% of small landlords who:
- Want better tools
- Don't want to give up 8-10% of revenue
- Are willing to use software to reduce risk instead of paying to transfer it
